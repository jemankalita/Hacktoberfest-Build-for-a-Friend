The problem

He writes everything down. Nobody can read it.

My friend takes notes in every single class. Then, the night before an exam, he photographs them and asks an AI chatbot to summarize them. It can't read his handwriting.

Worse, when an AI does read most of it, the mistakes look right. In our first test, a book title became a different word, "his" became "her", and an exam-relevant date simply disappeared. A tired student would never notice.

A general-purpose chatbot can't be taught one person's handwriting. A small open model on his own laptop, with a memory of his corrections, can.

How it learns

Correct it a few times. It remembers.

No retraining, no GPU farm. Every fix you make becomes memory that shapes how the next page is read.

Snap a page

Drop in photos of his notes. An open vision model reads them on your laptop, and you watch the text appear live.

Fix what it missed

Words it wasn't sure about are highlighted, so checking a page takes a minute, not twenty.

It remembers

His names, terms and usual misreads are fed into the next page. A misread fixed twice is corrected automatically from then on.

Under the hood

It learns his handwriting, not just his words.

Corrections teach it what he meant. His word library and personal model teach it how he writes.

A library of his handwriting

Every corrected word is located on the page, cut out as an image and stored with its correct label, building a dataset of exactly how he shapes each letter.

A personal model, trained locally

His word library is used to fine-tune a personal version of the open model right on the laptop, so it reads his "w" and "u" the way he means them.

Knows his slips from its mistakes

Before saving, each change is marked as a misread to learn or a slip in his notes to skip, and real words he uses are flagged for review instead of silently replaced.

Why open matters

His notes. His laptop. His model.

Every piece that makes HandNotes work is open: the Gemma model, the Ollama runtime, and this code.

Private

Photos of his notes never leave the laptop. No uploads, no account, no one else's server.

Offline

Works in a hostel room with bad Wi-Fi the night before an exam. Even the fonts are bundled.

Free to run

No subscription and no API bill, so a lazy student will actually keep using it.

Yours to change

Swap the model with one setting, tweak the prompt, or fine-tune on the corrections later.
